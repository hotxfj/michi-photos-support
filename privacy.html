Privacy Policy

How Michi Photos handles your media, protection, and optional iCloud backups.

Updated October 16, 2026

Your photos stay yours

Michi Photos is developed by Jeff Xu. The app stores your imported photos, videos, previews, and album information in encrypted files on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your media, passwords, or recovery codes. The app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Protection you choose

Face ID or Touch ID and an album password are optional. Apple handles biometric verification; the app receives a result, not your face or fingerprint data. Each album has its own encryption keys. Turning off the unlock screen does not remove file encryption. Keep your device passcode and recovery codes secure.

Optional iCloud backup

If you enable backup or choose Back Up to iCloud, encrypted media, previews, album information, and encrypted recovery material are sent to your private iCloud database through Apple CloudKit. Operational records include random identifiers, file sizes, integrity hashes, and backup dates. This uses your iCloud storage and Apple account. We cannot browse your private database from our developer account. Apple processes iCloud service data under its own privacy policy.

Import, export, and deletion

The app reads the files you select through Photos, Files, or a share sheet. Importing makes a copy and does not delete the source. Exporting creates decrypted copies at the destination you choose, where that destination’s protection applies. Deletion in Michi Photos is permanent. If a file was backed up, its cloud deletion is queued until the app can contact iCloud; turning off automatic backup does not erase existing cloud copies. Uninstalling the app does not itself delete iCloud copies.

Diagnostics and contact

The app keeps limited transfer diagnostics on your device, such as stages and error codes. It does not automatically send them to us. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request and retain it only as needed for support or legal obligations. Do not send private media, passwords, or recovery codes. You can email us to request deletion of your support correspondence.

Your choices

You can use the app without iCloud, change protection in Settings, export your files, or delete them in the app. Download cloud-only originals before removing a backup you still need. The developer cannot reset your album password or recover missing keys or files. Questions about this policy can be sent to hotxfj@icloud.com.
