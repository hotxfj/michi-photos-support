Help & Contact

A personal home for your photos and videos. Find answers and get in touch.

Start with your photos

Tap Add to import from Photos or Files. You can also share a supported photo or video to Michi from another app. Importing copies the file; the original stays where it was.

Protect an album

Open Settings to turn on Face ID or Touch ID, an album password, or both. With both enabled, biometric verification comes first. Save the recovery code when you choose protection or iCloud backup. Each album has its own code.

Keep two albums separate

Enable Secondary Album in Settings. It has the same features as Primary Album, with separate content and protection. Use Copy or Move to transfer selected items; a protected destination must be unlocked. You can choose which album opens at launch.

Back up, then save device space

iCloud Backup saves encrypted copies to your own iCloud account. It does not automatically remove originals. Manage Storage shows backup counts and lets you release verified originals or download them again. Automatic optimization is optional: while the app is active, it checks periodically for backed-up originals not viewed for seven days. Manually retained downloads are kept.

If a transfer pauses

iOS controls background time and may pause a transfer after a long lock or when resources are limited. Reopen the app and choose Continue Transfer. Verified portions are retained. Check your connection and available iCloud space if it cannot continue. Force-quitting can interrupt background work.

Restore a backup

Use Restore from iCloud in Settings with the same iCloud account and that album’s saved recovery code. Only successfully backed-up content can be restored. Backups are not live synchronization between devices. Recovery codes do not replace a media backup.

Export or delete

Export to Photos or Files, including an external drive supported by iOS. Exported files are decrypted so other apps can open them. Deleting in Michi is permanent and does not delete the source in Photos. Before deleting cloud backups, download any originals you want to keep.
